Discover key features and user interface of Simple Flashcards

Dictionary Search & Import
Search 104k+ offline headwords and easily add any word directly to your flashcard sets.
Instant Auto Card Creation
Paste a list of words to automatically generate definitions, IPA pronunciations, and example sentences.
Offline Camera & Photo OCR
Scan physical textbooks or photos to extract vocabulary words with zero manual typing.
System-Wide Quick Add
Highlight text in any external app (browser, Kindle, PDF) to create enriched flashcards in 1 tap.
Ad-Free & Privacy Focused
No user accounts, no subscriptions, no ads, and no internet required for complete peace of mind.
Real-World Context & Meanings
Study with comprehensive definitions, authentic example sentences, and native audio pronunciation.
Two-Gesture Active Recall
Frictionless flashcard study model: tap to flip front/back, swipe left or right to navigate cards.
Pre-Bundled Starter Sets
Start studying immediately with bundled IELTS, TOEFL, SAT, and CEFR (A1–C1) word decks.
